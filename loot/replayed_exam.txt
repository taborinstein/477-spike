PROBLEM 1 (Graph theory)
Prove that every tree on n >= 2 vertices has at least two leaves.

PROBLEM 2 (Number theory)
Compute 7^(644) mod 645 using repeated squaring. Show each step.

PROBLEM 3 (Linear algebra)
Let A be a 3x3 real matrix with eigenvalues 1, 2, and 2.
Is A necessarily diagonalizable? Justify.

PROBLEM 4 (Algorithms)
Give the tight asymptotic running time of the recurrence
    T(n) = 2 T(n/2) + n log n
and prove your bound.

PROBLEM 5 (Probability)
A fair coin is flipped until two consecutive heads appear.
Find the expected number of flips.
